Good to Know

Questions Couples Ask Us

What's your photography style?

We combine candid wedding photography with fine-art and contemporary portraiture, capturing genuine emotions alongside timeless, beautifully composed photographs.

What's covered on the wedding day?

Our coverage can include getting ready, rituals, ceremonies, family and couple portraits, receptions and candid guest moments, documenting your celebration from start to finish.

How do we prepare for a pre-wedding or wedding shoot?

We'll guide you on outfits, locations, timing and the overall plan beforehand. You can relax, be yourselves and let us capture the moments naturally.

How far in advance should we book?

We recommend booking around six months ahead, especially for popular dates. However, it's always worth checking with us, even if your wedding is approaching soon.
